Privacy Policy

In effect from 25 September 2026 · Interviews for iPhone

← Interviews Italiano

In two lines

Interviews collects nothing. No account, no advertising, no usage analytics, no third-party services. The app runs entirely on your iPhone and sends data to nobody, not even to the person who wrote it.

Who the controller is

Claudia Napolitano, reachable at napolitano.claudia@icloud.com. Since no personal data ever arrives, there is no processing of your data to speak of.

What stays on your device

The app saves on your iPhone only what it needs to work: the tracks you picked, your starting level, XP and rank, your streak and completed days, right and wrong answers, lessons read and the reminder time. They live in the app's own storage (UserDefaults), never leave the device, and contain no name, email, location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers.

No network

The app has no feature that connects to the Internet: questions, lessons and images ship inside the app from the moment you install it. There are no servers of ours to reach, so there are no logs, no collected IP addresses and no cookies.

Backups

If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or to a computer, the app's progress goes into that backup along with the rest of the phone. That backup is yours, handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy: we have no access to it.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications: your iPhone schedules them, they do not come from a server, and no push token is ever created or sent. You grant the permission and you can take it back whenever you like, from Settings › Notifications › Interviews or by turning the switch off in your Profile.

No tracking

Interviews does not track your activity across other apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it has no reason to, and shares nothing with data brokers. It bundles no third-party SDK of any kind.

Deleting everything

Profile › Reset progress immediately erases your streak, stats, XP and mistakes. Deleting the app removes every saved trace. You do not need to ask us for anything: no copies exist anywhere else.

Your rights

European law (GDPR) gives you access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing to exercise them against here; if you would like to write anyway, the address is napolitano.claudia@icloud.com. You can also complain to your national data protection authority — in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

Children

The app covers technical job-interview material and is meant for a general audience. Since it collects data from nobody, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and at https://cldnpl.github.io/interviews/privacy.html, with the date at the top updated. Substantial changes will be called out in the release notes of the update.

Contact

Any question about this policy: napolitano.claudia@icloud.com.
